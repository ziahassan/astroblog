what is, in a poetic sense, vertical time?



In a poetic sense, vertical time refers to a concept of time that is not linear or chronological but rather momentous and experiential. It emphasizes depth and intensity of individual moments, experiences or feelings. It's about delving deeper into a single moment and exploring all its complexities, rather than moving forward in a horizontal timeline. This concept allows poets to explore themes of memory, consciousness, and the human experience in more depth.
