#coachingtool 

Rather than go with your mind, go with your heart.

Hand over heart... 

"Higher, wiser, future-self... what do you really know to be true about this situation?"

This, listen. The answer might come in whispers, in calm talk, or body sensations.

This is what [[jen lara]] did for me when she coached me about sending [[Dezi]] back to daycare in June 2020.

This can be done with writing, kind of like [[morning pages 1]] with more intention.

From [[Marie Forleo]]
