### BIS (Behavioral Inhibition System)
- **Function**: The BIS is responsible for sensitivity to punishment, non-reward, and novel situations. It regulates avoidance behavior.
- **Emotional Responses**: It is associated with feelings of anxiety, fear, and apprehension. When activated, it can lead to cautious or inhibited behavior.
- **Neurobiological Basis**: The BIS is thought to involve brain regions such as the hippocampus and amygdala, which are associated with processing risk and fear.
