[[George W. Bush]] became a bit obsessive about Education as his main priority first year in office.

The aftermath of 9/11 helped; moving quickly and decisively on _any_ topic was what people wanted.

Parties came together and collaborated on the NCLB; it felt like bipartisanship at its finest. 

Kids should be held to the same high standards.

Congress couldn't find the bipartisanship to reauthorize this six years later, which allowed problems to accumulate.

We have to recreate a sense of purpose and principle that drove people in previous times when there was more bipartisanship.

[[Education is a pendulum swing between whole student and outcomes]].
