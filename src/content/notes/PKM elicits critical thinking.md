[[PKM]] , personal knowledge management, is a way to interact with the various incoming media in our lives.

YouTuber [[morganeua]] points out that [[Zettelkasten/Cards/Concepts/Critical Thinking|Critical Thinking]] is something we often talk about but don't ever define. 

I used to define it as aligning your actions with your values, and I still believe that's true. But there also needs to be some element of [[thinking]], and therefore interacting with knowledge through a system such as a PKM system is an example of critical thinking as long as you take action.
