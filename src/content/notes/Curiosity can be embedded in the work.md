The cartoonist [[Al Schmidt]] puts his daughter's name in his work, hidden somewhere within.

But he also put a number next to his signature, indicating how many times his daughter's name appears in the work.

This creates:
- [[mystery]]
- [[curiosity]] through [[clues]]
- [[romance]]
- [[noticing]]

Heard about this from [[Helen Molesworth]] on the [[Time Sensitive Podcast]].
