Why might the following statement be true?

There is no such thing as educational theories, it draws from all the other disciplines

This statement reflects the idea that educational theories do not exist in isolation but instead are a synthesis of various other disciplines. Education is a multidisciplinary field that draws upon psychological, sociological, philosophical, and anthropological perspectives to understand how people learn and develop. Theories regarding effective teaching methods, student motivation, curriculum development, educational equity, and many more aspects all draw from research and theories in these various disciplines. Hence, it could be said that there is no such thing as "pure" educational theory - it's all influenced by other areas of study.
