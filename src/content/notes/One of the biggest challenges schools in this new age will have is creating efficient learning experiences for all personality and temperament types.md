For instance, introverted students may benefit from more individualized study time or online learning platforms that allow them to learn at their own pace. On the other hand, extroverted students may thrive in group activities or interactive classroom settings.

Furthermore, some students thrive in structured environments where expectations are clearly outlined while others prefer a more flexible approach where they can explore topics independently.
