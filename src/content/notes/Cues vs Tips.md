I always used to get thrown off by this - [[tips]] vs [[cues]].

Tips are just things that worked for you that you're now imposing on someone else (could be useful).

Cues are when you are _training_ someone to think differently when they're doing their craft. 

A tip: increase your weight every week when you're doing Squats and deadlifts.

A cue: shove your knees out on the way down when you squat.

Both can be useful; cues help you get better faster.
