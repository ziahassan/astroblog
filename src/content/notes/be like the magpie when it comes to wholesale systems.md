Instead of subscribing wholesale to the [[The-Bullet-Journal-Method]] or [[Getting-Things-Done]] or whatever it is, pick and choose and stick to what works, but only when it works. This is how the magpie is. Otherwise you are just adopting someone else's system wholesale and judging it based on its merits as a whole system rather than how it fits into yours. Same goes for [[parenting]] techniques. This idea came from a [[westenberg]] video.
