what if there was a play day?

You do everything you normally except you ask yourself what it would look like to play before you did it.

So, brushing your teeth could become a teeth brushing song/dance.

Writing an essay could become an index card twirl and whirl on the floor with ideas.

Reading a research paper might become turning the main idea of the paper into a haiku (something ChatGPT could do, but we'll do as humans to really internalize it).
