> “He did this to amuse people. To amuse himself. He played all the time, like a child. When Matisse and Picasso said, 'Draw like a child, they meant use your whole mastery in simplicity. What they meant was to be pure in all of your work. In Joseph Conrad's _The Secret Agent_, which is a very great book, one of the characters is a boy who sweeps the floor, a menial job, but how does he do it? He plays. That is how he enjoys it. He does it with his entire being. There is no other way to do anything.

[[Hedda Sterne]] by way of [[Zettelkasten/People/Austin Kleon]] (as usual).

This reminds me of something [[Alan Watts]] once said about doing the dishes. You get into the "groove" of it. And that's basically all of life.
