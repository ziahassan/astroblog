Don't fall prey to the idea that you must finish what you start.

Yes, there is value in getting past what [[Seth Godin]] refers to as [[the dip]], but when you've watched 3-4 episodes or read chapters of something and it's just not connecting, perhaps it isn't worth your time to continue. I've heard [[Zettelkasten/People/Austin Kleon|Austin Kleon]] talk about abandoning books, too.
