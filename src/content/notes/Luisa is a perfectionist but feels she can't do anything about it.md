Luisa in [[Encanto]] is complaining about how everything is always handed to her and she's expected to do everything for everyone.

It's a great example of how you can be aware of your own saboteurs ([[perfectionist]]) but still feel trapped by them. No one was enslaving Luisa or making her do all this stuff. 

She kept doing it anyway because that was now her [[identity]], because she listened to the lies from her [[saboteur]].
