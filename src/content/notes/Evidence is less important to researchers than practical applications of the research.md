This phrase suggests that for some researchers, the ultimate value of their work lies not in the theoretical or empirical evidence they gather, but in how this research can be practically applied to solve real-world problems or improve people's lives. While evidence is crucial for establishing the validity of their research, these researchers might prioritize creating tangible benefits and changes through their work. However, this doesn't mean that they completely disregard evidence; rather, they view it as a means to an end - the end being practical application.
