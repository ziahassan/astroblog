I think [[Jack Black]] makes this point really well; [[Tenacious D]] would have never been a serious band because them being a serious band makes them blend in with everything else. They have to have the cussing, the innuendo, the weirdness in order for it to make sense.

And [[If-You-Let-AI-Do-Your-Writing-I-Will-Come-to-Your-House-and-Kill-You]], [[Sam Kriss]] makes this same point by continually saying that he will come to your house and kill you if you use AI, a way of making the point that only the strongest most visceral raw emotion in writing will come across as human.

Time to start acting like.
