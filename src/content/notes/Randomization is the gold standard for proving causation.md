This is related to the [[Covid19 Pandemic]] in that we did a lot of studies and it was the randomized ones that were seen as the most relevant.

The gold standard for studies of this nature are [[randomized control trials]]

More specifically, it helps ensure [[statistical equivalence]] on average. The groups are equivalent, but _on average._

You can use statistics to check for statistical equivalence.

But it doesn't mean that, for the specific goal you have, this is the best type of study. Sometimes a [[quasirandom study]] could be even better.
