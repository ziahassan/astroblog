Key Factors That Make Comic Sans More Readable:

1. Letter Shapes
- More casual, handwritten appearance that mimics natural writing
- Clear distinctions between similar letters (like 'b' and 'd')
- Less formal and rigid compared to traditional fonts

2. Spacing and Design
- Letters are slightly irregular, like natural handwriting
- Good spacing between characters
- Rounded edges and informal style

3. Benefits for Specific Groups:
- Helpful for people with dyslexia due to unique letter shapes
- Easier for young children learning to write
- More approachable for casual reading

However, it's important to note:
- Professional settings generally prefer more formal fonts
- Not everyone agrees Comic Sans is more readable
- Context matters - appropriate for casual use but not formal documents

The font's readability comes from its informal, handwritten-style design that mirrors natural writing patterns, though its use should be considered based on the specific context and audience.
