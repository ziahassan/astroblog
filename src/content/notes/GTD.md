Methodology by [[David Allen]] that has superpowered my life for the past few years.

I will use this page to capture everything I know about it.

# Core Principles
- Capture everything. Don't worry about doing _any_ sort of processing. Big or small, it doesn't matter the weight of the task. Just put it into a brain dump, or an inbox of some kind.
- Clarify the tasks later. You want a verb to start the task.
