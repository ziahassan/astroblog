> Trauma dumping refers to the act of sharing traumatic or emotionally heavy information with someone without their consent or without considering their emotional capacity to handle it. This can unintentionally transfer stress or anxiety onto the other person, potentially triggering them or causing emotional harm. While it’s important for individuals to express their feelings and share their experiences, it’s equally important to ensure that the recipient is prepared and willing to receive such information.
