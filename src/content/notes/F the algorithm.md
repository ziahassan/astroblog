# Performance Enhancements
- Human led messaging. Doesn't mean that you need to write every word yourself but you own the tone, direction, the voice, etc.
- Think about what your clients are experiencing and use THOSE for your hooks. Not the robot.
- Give examples of our own work, train it to sound like you (not sure I totally agree with this, but ok)
- Get rid of words that are around platform-specific stuff with more general stuff, like gif, meme dump, all text, meme video, single image, lofi video, long form text, long form video, etc.
- Choose 1-2 and go all in on them! For all of your brand's social media content. Definitions of these are in the workbook (Google Docs).
- Repurpose your content
- Choose these based on your talents, your resources, and the platforms you're on right now
