Explain intelligent tutoring systems in one paragraph or less



Intelligent tutoring systems are computer-based learning programs that use artificial intelligence to provide personalized instruction, guidance, or feedback to students without the need for human intervention. These systems adapt to the learner's knowledge level and learning pace, identifying areas of weakness and tailoring instruction accordingly. They can simulate the one-on-one interaction of a human tutor, making them valuable tools in education technology.
