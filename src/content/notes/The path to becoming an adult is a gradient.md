When you're a kid, you're a tiny drunk adult.

And when you're an adult, you're a tall child.

The path to becoming an adult is a gradient. There isn't a switch that gets pulled on the day you turn 18 that qualifies you to make "adult decisions."

It is a gradual, painstaking process. 

In the end, what determines our "adultness?" Does it mean that we become less emotional? Less petulent? 

We never lose our childishness. Not completely, anyway. 

[[We're practicing for who we want to be]].
