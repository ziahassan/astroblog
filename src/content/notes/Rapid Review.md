A rapid review is related to a [[systematic literature review]] except we leave out a few of the features that make longer systematic reviews take a long time, in order to save time.

We trade precision for speed, and in most cases, it works out just fine as long as we specify that that's what we're doing.

Tools used for screening process in Rapid Reviews are:

[[AS Review]] and also another big one is [[Covidence]]. You could probably do most of the work in Covidence but AS Review seems to have some machine learning component that helps.
