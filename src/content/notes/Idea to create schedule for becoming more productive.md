A book for people who have _never_ implmented a productivity system of any kind to get things done. They are turned off by all the crazy stuff, and just want simplicity. What would be the principles?

1. Works for your brain
2. Relieves pressure/tension
3. Capture tool
4. Scale from simple to complex (maybe you get to stop at whatever point makes sense)]]


Potential chapters/sections:
1. A SINGLE INDEX CARD: Start with nothing but an index card. Sit down to work and write down the most important thing that comes to mind. Do that first.
2. TWO INDEX CARDS: Write down on one card the top 3 things you need to accomplish that day. Write the most important one on on the other card.
3. THREE INDEX CARDS: Your list of your top 3-5 things to complete that day. A schedule of when you will do each thing. Third index card, the thing you are going to do first. Maybe write what you will do first, then plan the rest of the day's activities on the third card.
4. FOUR+ INDEX CARDS:
	1. Brain dump - everything you can possibly think of that needs to be done on the first card, front and back. 
	2. Card 2 - select the 3-5 most important tasks
	3. Pick the ONE thing.
	4. Schedule activities on another card.
	5. Do the ONE thing.
