This week is all about mindset.

You'll want to pay attention to this, because mindset is everything.

It's not a cute buzzword. People who develop a winner's mindset do it intentionally, by doing various kinds of self-work. 

Changing your mindset can be as easy as flipping a switch. You can simply decide to see things differently.

Mindset can make the difference between doing work you love and settling for whatever you can get.

The switch we want to flip today is chasing vs. attracting.

What happens when you chase, for example a dog (let's call him Fido)?
- You run after Fido.
- Fido is too fast for you.
- You get tired.
- You complain that Fido is just too fast to catch. 
- You keep chasing Fido.
- Maybe you'll get lucky and catch Fido.
- Fido gets away.
- Rinse and repeat.

That's what chasers do. What would an attractor do?
- Learn Fido's favorite food.
- Hold some of the food in your hand.
- Fido comes to you; no chasing required.
- Fido stays put, as long as you're giving Fido what he's looking for.

The same is true for the job hunt.

Wow, even that phrase "job hunt" is chase-oriented, isn't it?!

It's easy to convince yourself that you're not in a position of power, when it comes to finding a career or job that you love.

It's easy to convince yourself that you're going to have to roam the streets and knock on doors until someone gives you work that satisfies your desires.

But this is a scarcity mindset. This is chasing Fido.

Instead, get clear on your values. Tame your negative self-talk. Figure out what you stand for. Then stand for it. 

In other words, be an attractor.

People, employers, companies... they're simply searching for others who share their values. 

They'll hire an attractor over a chaser any day of the week.
