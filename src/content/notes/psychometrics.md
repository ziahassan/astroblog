Metrics related to psychological traits.



1. Big Five Personality Traits: This model measures an individual's openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism.

2. Myers-Briggs Type Indicator: This is a questionnaire that identifies 16 personality types based on four dichotomies: introversion/extraversion, sensing/intuition, thinking/feeling, and judging/perception.

3. Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory (MMPI): This is a psychological test that assesses personality traits and psychopathology.

4. Beck Depression Inventory (BDI): This self-report scale measures the severity of depression in adolescents and adults.

5. State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI): It measures state (temporary condition) and trait (long-standing quality) anxiety.

6. Stanford-Binet Intelligence Scales: These scales measure intelligence in children and adults with five factors of cognitive abilities in mind: fluid reasoning, knowledge, quantitative reasoning, visual-spatial processing, and working memory.

7. Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale (WAIS): It is used to measure an adult's intellectual ability.

8. Rorschach Inkblot Test: This projective test uses inkblots to understand a person's thought processes and personality characteristics.

9. Thematic Apperception Test (TAT): In this projective test, individuals make up stories about ambiguous scenes to reveal their unconscious thoughts, feelings, and desires.

10. Millon Clinical Multiaxial Inventory (MCMI): It assesses personality disorders and clinical syndromes in adults.

11. Emotional Intelligence Scale: Measures the ability to perceive emotions in oneself or others accurately and manage one's own emotions effectively.
   
12. 16PF Questionnaire: A self-report assessment tool that measures the 16 personality factors identified by Raymond Cattell.
   
13. NEO Personality Inventory-Revised (NEO PI-R): Assesses the big five personality factors (neuroticism, extraversion, openness, agreeableness, and conscientiousness).
   
14. Hamilton Rating Scale for Depression (HRSD): A 21-item rating scale used to measure the severity of depressive symptoms.

15. Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI): Measures burnout in three areas: emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and personal accomplishment.
