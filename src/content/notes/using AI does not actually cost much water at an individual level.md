You use about 16k liters of water a day; ChatGPT prompt using like 2ml.

Which means that in order to get to 16k you'd have to send like 8k prompts a day, but then you probably aren't doing other things that lead to water consumption, which means maybe it's _better to use ChatGPT all day than just live your regular life, if you care about the environment._

This argument was made by [[Andy Masley]] on the [[hard fork]] podcast.
