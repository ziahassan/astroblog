Why we love treasure/ scavenger hunts from a psychological perspective 
 #tvz


1. Satisfaction of Curiosity: One of the primary psychological reasons behind our love for scavenger hunts is the satisfaction of our innate curiosity. Humans are naturally inclined towards exploration and discovery, and a treasure hunt perfectly caters to this instinct.

2. Sense of Accomplishment: Successfully finding clues and decoding them gives us a sense of achievement. This feeling is highly rewarding psychologically and encourages us to further participate in such activities.

3. Problem-solving Skills: Scavenger hunts often involve puzzles or riddles that need to be solved to move forward. This challenges our problem-solving skills, which can be thrilling and satisfying to successfully navigate.

4. Competition & Cooperation: Scavenger hunts can also involve teamwork and competition, which are both strong motivators from a psychological perspective. The drive to win, coupled with the camaraderie formed within teams, can make the experience more enjoyable.

5. Adventure & Novelty: The novelty of embarking on an adventure and experiencing something out of the ordinary also appeals to many people on a psychological level.

6. Intrinsic Motivation: The fun and excitement involved in scavenger hunts fuel our intrinsic motivation - participating for the sheer joy of it rather than any external rewards.

7. Learning & Creativity: Scavenger hunts often require creative thinking and offer opportunities for learning - both aspects that contribute positively to our cognitive development and overall well-being.

8. Stress Relief: Engaging in fun activities like treasure hunts can provide a mental break from daily routines, thereby relieving stress and boosting mood.

9. Connection with Others: Participating in a group activity like a scavenger hunt helps build social bonds, facilitating feelings of connection and belongingness, which are fundamental human needs according to Maslow's hierarchy of needs.
  
10. Memory Formation: The unique experiences during scavenger hunts help form vivid memories, contributing to personal narratives that we value.
  
Overall, scavenger or treasure hunts offer a combination of mental stimulation, social interaction, and fun that can be deeply satisfying and enjoyable from a psychological perspective.
