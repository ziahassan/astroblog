The idea of a concept is just a densely linked network of smaller ideas. They were put together by someone at some time, but all [[information]] and [[knowledge management]] works this way, in a modular way at first coming together to create a concept.

Ex: [[Evernote]] style note-taking -> Flat file system like [[NValt]] -> [[Drafts]] (just capture) -> [[Zettelkasten/Cards/Concepts/Obsidian]] ([[PKM]])

One could also argue that the emergence of [[Markdown]] was part of that journey too.

Source: [[Peepnbrick]], [How to Achieve More While Studying Less](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MYJsGksojms)
