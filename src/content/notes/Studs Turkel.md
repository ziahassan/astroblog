- Studs Terkel was an American author, historian, actor and broadcaster known for his oral histories of common Americans.
- He won the Pulitzer Prize for General Non-Fiction in 1985 for his book "The Good War", which is about World War II as told through interviews with servicemen and civilians.
- Terkel hosted a daily radio show in Chicago from 1952 to 1997 where he interviewed a wide range of guests, from everyday workers to famous figures in arts, politics and science.
- His other notable works include "Working", in which he interviewed people about their jobs; "Hard Times", an oral history of the Great Depression; and "Race", a look at racial issues in America.
- Terkel was also a prominent activist who spoke out on various issues including civil rights, labor rights and anti-war movements.
