This is in reference to musicians like [[Herbie Hancock]]. [[Brian Eno]] tells the story about how he watched Hancock play and how he would play a melody, then remove some of the notes, and then remove some more, until only a chiseled, perfect melody remained.

[[a good melody comes out immediately]].

This makes me think of [[John Cage]] as well as [[Michaelangelo]] - both artists who made sense of emptiness, and stillness.
