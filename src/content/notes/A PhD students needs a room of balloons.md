🎈
The metaphor is that I should be living in a room of balloons rather than a tightly packed trunk of.a car on vacation.

This is a space for some productivity ideas around this concept.

First, [[Andy Stapleton]] has a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmsPlCjjbnU) about a workflow to be productive as a [[PhD]] student. How to collect, read, annotate, analyze, and synthesize.

[[Larry McEnery]] talks about the [process of academic writing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLPCdDp_LE0&t=2347s), with the main messages being: 
1. [[tell the experts they're wrong]].
2. [[Knowledge is a conversation moving through time]]
3. [[Your writing must create value or it is useless]]

[[Tara Brabazon]] has a process for reading any article:
1. Read the abstract, intro, and conclusion. 
2. Answer two questions:
	1. What is the argument this article is making?
	2. How is it valuable to _my_ reserach question?
3. Read the articles that are most valuable, toss the ones that aren't.
