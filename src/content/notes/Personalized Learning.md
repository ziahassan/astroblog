Personalized learning can be simply defined in 5 bullets as 

1. A tailored educational approach: Personalized learning focuses on tailoring the educational content and pace according to the individual student's learning style, interests, and skills.
   
2. Student-centered methodology: This learning method puts students at the center of the learning process, allowing them to take control of their own education.

3. Use of technology: Personalized learning often uses digital tools and online resources to adapt educational content to each student's needs.

4. Regular assessment and feedback: Assessments are used not only for grading but also for understanding a student's progress, challenges, and potential for growth. Feedback is given regularly to guide students in their learning journey.

5. Flexibility in learning environment: Personalized education recognizes that students may learn better in various settings. It allows flexibility in terms of when, where and how the learning takes place – whether it’s at home, at school or through blended learning methods.
