The pandemic was a swirl of competing values.

Those who valued autonomy were likely angry about all of the new rules imposed on them, both socially and societally.

Those who were parents had a different calculus than those who weren't.

Those who were lonely before hand were loneliness still, and maybe had more despair now as a result of the endless tunnel ahead.

Maybe, everyone had their own values turned against them in the pandemic.

It was a different sort of hard for every single person.

Like a spiritual journey that everyone must endure.
