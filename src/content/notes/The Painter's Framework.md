Summarize, detail, summarize

For better [[communication]].

# Prep 

draw with pencil on the canvas, think about what you're going to say, roughly.

Who is the audience, what do you want them to do? When do you wan them to do it by?

What's the point I'm trying to get across.

# Paint
broad colors, no details staying high level.

Then add in more detail to the parts that matter. Where do you want to focus on?

# Summary
Bring it all together - what is the synthesis?

Link it to next steps.o
