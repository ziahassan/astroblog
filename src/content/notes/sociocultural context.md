Sociocultural context, A term from [[Jonathan Plucker]] that describes:

The societal and cultural factors that influence an individual or group's behavior, attitudes, perceptions, and worldview. It includes aspects such as norms, values, beliefs, language, customs, traditions and social structures that shape a person's identity and experiences. The sociocultural context can greatly impact how individuals interpret and react to different situations or phenomena. It is a crucial concept in various fields such as sociology, anthropology, psychology and education.
