Why people are buying more physical things in mid2020's



There could be several reasons behind this trend:

1. Pandemic Impact: The COVID-19 pandemic has forced people to stay at home more than ever before which might have increased the demand for physical items like home decor, fitness equipment, cooking tools, etc.

2. Online Shopping Growth: With the rise of e-commerce platforms, buying physical items has become easier and more convenient. 

3. Emotional Satisfaction: Owning a physical object often provides emotional satisfaction. For example, some people prefer having a physical book or vinyl record over a digital version.

4. Sustainability Trend: With the increasing awareness of sustainability and environmental issues, many people prefer to buy durable goods that will last longer, instead of disposable or single-use items.

5. Nostalgia and Collectability: Certain physical items like vinyl records, vintage clothing or antiques can have nostalgia value or be considered collectibles.

6. Home Improvement Projects: As people spend more time at home due to remote working conditions, they may choose to invest in their living spaces through furniture, appliances and other household goods.

7. Investment in Personal Activities: Due to restrictions on travel and public activities during the pandemic period, many individuals may have chosen to invest in personal hobbies or activities which require the purchase of physical items such as musical instruments, art supplies or sporting equipment.
  
8. Economic Stability: Depending on individual economic stability during this period, spending habits could have varied greatly with some choosing to spend more on physical items for comfort during uncertain times.
   
Remember these are possible explanations and trends can vary based on geographic location, individual preferences and other factors.
