I used to hate it when this happened.

I'd be in the vicinity of someone south Asian, and after a few niceties, they say: "so where are you from?"

My typical response is "Rockville." 

But then they hit me with "where are you _really_ from?"

"My parents are from Pakistan."

This happened today with the handyman who was helping me mount the ceiling projector. 

For the first time, I wondered why people do this. It's not just to make conversation. They crave familiarity. They crave contact with someone from their tribe. 

If I can be that for someone, then I suppose I will.
