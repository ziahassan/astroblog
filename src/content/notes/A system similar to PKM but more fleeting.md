It works like this:

Every thought that comes in, you label as:
- Observation (something you notice)
- Realization (something you learned)
- Insight (something you teach

Ex:
Dezi playing the [[Fiction or Non Fiction]] game. Observe that this is an interesting activity for a child to engage with.

Realize that never ask yourself this about your beliefs.

Insight: teach people how to ask this before they accept their beliefs. This is the production part.
