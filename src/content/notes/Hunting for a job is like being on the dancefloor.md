There will be people who ghost you. Leads that seem like they're going somewhere until they drop out of existence. Maybe even offers that are later rescinded or even fake/scams.

No one can tell you how long you'll be on the dance floor. One thing's for sure though: The more time you spend on the floor, the better you become at dancing.
