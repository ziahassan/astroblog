What is security through transparency?

Security through transparency is a concept that means making the systems, protocols, and processes openly accessible and understandable for users, ensuring they can trust in its security. It involves revealing details about the encryption methods, privacy policies, data handling techniques etc., so that independent experts or users can evaluate and verify the security measures in place. This transparency can help to build trust among users while also potentially revealing vulnerabilities that could be exploited if not addressed.
