[[Jaron Lanier]], a renowned computer scientist, describes BUMMER as an acronym for "Behaviors of Users Modified, and Made into an Empire for Rent". In his book "Ten Arguments for Deleting Your Social Media Accounts Right Now", Lanier uses BUMMER to refer to the business model of some tech companies like Facebook and Google. This model involves the manipulation of users' behaviors through algorithms for the benefit of advertisers and other paying entities.
