As far as I know this term came from [[Jason Gulya]] on LinkedIn.

The idea is that you can use [[AI]] to assist in your work to a certain point, and after that it feels like it's no longer yours.

Students and teachers alike have this barometer. 

We _think_ everyone will be tempted to just go all the way with these tools, but in the end, [[we want to feel like the creator of our own work]].
