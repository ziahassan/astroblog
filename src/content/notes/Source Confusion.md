Source Confusion
The ice cream guy and the dentist from my childhood blend together in my head.

In my head they are the same person.

This is apparently known as source confusion:

> The phenomenon you’re describing sounds like a form of **“source confusion”** or **“misattribution of memory.”** This occurs when a person remembers information but attributes it to the wrong source. In your case, the memory of the ice cream guy and the dentist have become intertwined, leading you to recall them as the same person in your childhood memories.
