I was once told that I have [[high quality thoughts 1]].

It's not an innate talent. I _did_ have quite a wide imagination as a child, and I still do, but I don't attribute this person's observations to my rich inner world.

It's about intention.

I am intentional about the inputs and outputs of my life. 

The inputs include:
- Movies I watch
- Books I read
- Podcasts I listen to 
- Lectures I hear, etc.

The outputs include:
- Podcasts I create
- Blog posts
- These notes
- Conversations
- Lesson Plans
- Coaching resources
- etc.

The in-between step is creating [[structures to think about the inputs]] (like [[creativity as gardening]]), and [[templates to create the outputs]].
