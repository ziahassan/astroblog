- Summary:: The goal of many AI organizations is to create artificial general intelligence (AGI), but this aim is often not critically examined. The authors argue that the push for AGI reflects outdated eugenics ideas, leading to harmful biases against marginalized groups. They suggest that researchers should focus on defined tasks with clear safety protocols instead of pursuing an undefined, all-knowing AGI.
