# On Ultra-Processed Content

![rw-book-cover](https://www.calnewport.com/favicon.ico)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Linda Adams]]
- Full Title: On Ultra-Processed Content
- Category: #articles
- Summary:: The text discusses the concept of ultra-processed food and draws parallels between processed food and digital media content. It highlights the addictive nature of ultra-processed foods and social media content, emphasizing the importance of choosing less processed alternatives for a healthier relationship with information consumption. The author suggests that avoiding highly processed digital content should be seen as a positive step towards reducing digital distractions and improving overall well-being.
