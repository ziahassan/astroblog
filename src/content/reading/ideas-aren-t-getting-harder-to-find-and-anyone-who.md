- Category: #articles
- Summary:: The article argues against the popular belief that ideas are getting harder to find and that progress in science is slowing down. The author asserts that while the number of researchers has increased, the output per researcher has gone down. They also argue that past ideas seem obvious in retrospect and that new discoveries replace and compress old ones. The author criticizes the professionalization of science, which may prevent revolutionary science and deter divergent thinkers from entering the field.

- The way to go higher, of course, is not to build taller elevators. It’s to invent hot air balloons. And when the helicopters can’t go any higher, the solution is rocket ships. And when the rocket ships can’t go any higher, the solution is something we haven’t invented yet.
