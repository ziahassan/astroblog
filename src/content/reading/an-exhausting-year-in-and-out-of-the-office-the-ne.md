- Category: #articles
- Summary:: After years of upheaval caused by the pandemic, knowledge workers are now experiencing a pervasive atmosphere of fatigue and exhaustion. The waves of disruption, such as the Great Resignation and the Remote-Work Wars, have left workers feeling worn out and dissatisfied. One major factor contributing to this exhaustion is the increase in digital communication, with workers spending close to sixty percent of their time on communication tools like email and videoconferencing. This constant stream of messages and meetings leads to mental fatigue and blurs the line between work and home. To address this issue, organizations need to implement significant changes, such as setting new ground rules for communication and reducing the reliance on digital tools. By making these reforms, it is possible to create a more sustainable and satisfying work environment for knowledge workers.
