- we understand that learning is much more like an act of revision. All of our students bring to our classes certain ideas about the world, and about our subjects, and these preconceptions are a crucial part of the process of learning new ideas. For a student to be taught, she must revise her current understanding to become a new understanding—it doesn’t just happen automatically.1 ([Location 201](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B096YSCW29&location=201))

([Location 206](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B096YSCW29&location=206))
