- Summary:: In "Thinking Through Statistics," John Levi Martin argues that traditional statistics often fails to address real problems because it relies on assumptions about the correct models. He suggests that instead of only trying to confirm theories, we should use data to eliminate false interpretations and explore multiple possibilities. By embracing ambiguity and focusing on robust patterns, we can improve our understanding of complex social phenomena.
