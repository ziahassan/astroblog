- Summary:: The document "Philosophy Everywhere" emphasizes the importance of integrating philosophy into education to encourage students to ask big questions and connect different subjects naturally. By allowing space for questions, fostering conversations, and embracing interdisciplinary learning through philosophy, the curriculum can become more meaningful and engaging for students. Encouraging philosophical understanding early on can lead to deeper comprehension and a more enriched educational experience, promoting critical thinking and exploration of fundamental concepts across various disciplines. The document advocates for a shift towards a curriculum that values wisdom, encourages questioning, and connects the subjects through philosophical inquiry.
