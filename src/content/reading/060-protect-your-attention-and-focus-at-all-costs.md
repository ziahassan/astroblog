- Summary:: Theo Stowell emphasizes the importance of curating the content you consume online to protect your focus and attention. He suggests being selective about who you follow, using tools to limit distractions, and saving content for later to make intentional choices about what you engage with. This approach helps ensure that the information you take in aligns with your goals and keeps you from being overwhelmed by irrelevant content.
