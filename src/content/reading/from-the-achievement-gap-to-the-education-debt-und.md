- Summary:: Gloria Ladson-Billings argues that the focus on the achievement gap in U.S. education should shift to the concept of "education debt," which reflects long-standing inequities faced by minority and disadvantaged students. This education debt encompasses historical, economic, sociopolitical, and moral dimensions that have contributed to educational disparities. Addressing this debt is crucial for creating a more equitable future for all students.
