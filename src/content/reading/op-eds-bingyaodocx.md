## Highlights
- The underrepresentation of women in STEM goes beyond individual career setbacks or the gender wage gap; more importantly, it stifles innovation and reinforces inequalities. In fields like engineering, a lack of diversity produces biased designs - from ill-fitting PPE for women to unsafe seatbelts for shorter drivers. Diverse teams are also proven to solve complex problems more effectively and drive innovation. If STEM is to develop solutions that serve everyone, it must reflect the diversity of the world it seeks to improve. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jbhmfhjxhv9272fwetq2737f))
- Universities must go beyond symbolic diversity efforts and create environments where women feel they belong, can succeed, and see futures for themselves in STEM. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jbezfcrkmnbk5xfzeqp522et))
