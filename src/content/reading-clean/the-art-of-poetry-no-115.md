- Summary:: Louise Glück, the Nobel Prize-winning poet, visited Claremont McKenna College in Southern California in 2021. Due to COVID, she was driven by a friend from Berkeley, where she rented a house during the winters. Glück packed food and CDs for the road trip and reminisced about listening to operas with her ex-husband. She talked openly about her girlhood and struggles with anorexia. Glück also shared her experience of winning the Nobel Prize and the ceremony in her backyard. The interview explores her influences, style, and different types of books she writes.
