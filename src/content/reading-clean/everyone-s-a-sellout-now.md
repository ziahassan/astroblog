## Metadata

- Full Title: Everyone’s a Sellout Now
- Category: #articles
- Summary:: The rise of personal branding and self-promotion on social media has led to an age where everyone must be an entrepreneur, even artists and writers. The internet has made it so that no matter who you are or what you do, you cannot escape the tyranny of the personal brand. Despite the fact that music, publishing, and film have always been for-profit industries, we still like to think of them as the work of singular geniuses whose motivations are purely creative. However, under the tyranny of algorithmic media distribution, artists, authors, and anyone whose work concerns itself with what it means to be human, now have to be entrepreneurs too.
