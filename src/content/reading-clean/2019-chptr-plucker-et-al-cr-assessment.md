- Summary:: Assessment of creativity is a complex and debated topic within the field of creativity and innovation. The difficulty in measuring creativity stems from definitional issues, but there is a long history of creativity assessment. Psychometric measures of creative processes have been used extensively, although there is criticism of traditional "creativity tests." Self-reports of activities and attainments are seen as a preferable technique for measuring creativity. Assessing creative products is also important, but receives less attention in the literature. The lack of scalable instruments and strategies for large group assessments is a major limitation. Overall, there are strengths and weaknesses in creativity assessment, and there is a need for original approaches to improve measurement.
