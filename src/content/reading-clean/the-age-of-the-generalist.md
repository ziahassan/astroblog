## Highlights
- When people tell you to build a niche audience in a specific topic, they usually don’t realize that’s not what they are doing.
  They say that, and then they go on to give random mindset tips, discuss their beliefs, post about their lives, and discuss their other interests. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxtbccfy0xjgv22bz7ygakq7))
- To make this simple:
  • **Choose a big goal** – Is it financial freedom? Is it a fit body? Is it self-actualization? The purpose of a value creator – someone focused on value and education rather than entertainment and looks – is to help people improve their mind, body, business, and relationships.
  • **List out skills, interests, and beliefs** – What do people need to know in order to reach that goal? The combination of these is what makes you unique.
  • **Frame everything you write through the lens of achieving that goal** – If you want to talk about basket weaving for some reason, do it, but talk about how it benefits your life as a fun hobby that helps with creativity and mental clarity. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxtbfxpf29kbs3hf9edka7br))
- Every 3-6 months, launch at least one free product and one paid product until you have a brand and business you are satisfied with. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hxwy8qsvqnvr2tct01n0h2jv))
