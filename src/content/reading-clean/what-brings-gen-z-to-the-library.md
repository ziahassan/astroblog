- Category: #articles
- Summary:: Gen Z, born between 1997 and 2012, are heavy online users but still value print and library visits. A study by the American Library Association highlights their preference for libraries as safe spaces with access to resources like Wi-Fi and tech equipment, which aligns with expectations carried into college. Libraries are adapting to meet Gen Z's needs by offering flexible spaces, diverse amenities, and accommodating technology demands while still providing access to print materials. Surveys and data analysis by organizations like Occuspace and brightspot help libraries understand and adjust to student preferences, showing a shift towards collaborative spaces and service partnerships within academic institutions.
