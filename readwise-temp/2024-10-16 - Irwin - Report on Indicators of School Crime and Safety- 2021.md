## Highlights
- Overall, throughout the last decade, several crime and safety issues have become less prevalent at elementary and secondary schools. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaanwmsry136zvnyypbp0czv))
- In contrast, there were a total of 93 school shootings with casualties at public and private elementary and secondary schools in 2020–21—the highest number since 2000–01 ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaap2mstrbbs9atxqr6asztr))
- In 2020, students ages 12–18 experienced 285,400 victimizations at school13 and 380,900 victimizations away from school. This translates to a rate of 11 victimizations per 1,000 students at school14
  and a rate of 15 victimizations per
  1,000 students away from school (figure 2). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaap8zr9gbm7th0bewm8gsjd))
- The percentage of students who reported being bullied at school during the school year was higher for students of Two or more races (37 percent) than for White students (25 percent) and Black students (22 percent); all these percentages were in turn higher than the percentage of Asian students (13 percent). Higher percentages of 6th-, 7th-, and 8th-graders reported being bullied at school during the school year in 2019 (ranging from 27 to 28 percent), compared with 9th-, 10th-, and 12th-graders (ranging from 16 to 19 percent).
