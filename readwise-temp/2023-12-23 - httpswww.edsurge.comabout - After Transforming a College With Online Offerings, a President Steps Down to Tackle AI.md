Summary: Paul LeBlanc, the president of Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU), is stepping down after transforming the institution with its online offerings. When LeBlanc first became president, SNHU had a small student population and faced an uncertain future. However, he made the decision to expand the university's online programs, which ended up being a successful move. Now, SNHU has one of the largest student populations in the country, with over 200,000 online students. After stepping down, LeBlanc plans to focus on using AI to reshape college teaching, specifically exploring how generative AI tools like ChatGPT can be utilized. He aims to bring big changes to higher education and believes that AI will have a significant impact in this field.
