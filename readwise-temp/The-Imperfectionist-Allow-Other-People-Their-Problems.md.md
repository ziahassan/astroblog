## Metadata
- Author: [[Oliver Burkeman]]
- Full Title: The Imperfectionist- Allow Other People Their Problems
- Category: #articles
- Summary:: The article discusses the idea that when you prioritize the things that truly matter to you, there will inevitably be people who have a problem with it. The author shares their own experience of feeling anxious about disappointing others and constantly trying to placate them. However, they come to the realization that other people's emotions are just one factor to consider when making choices about how to spend your time. Ultimately, it is important to recognize that other people's emotions are not your responsibility and that you have the freedom to make decisions that align with your own priorities.
