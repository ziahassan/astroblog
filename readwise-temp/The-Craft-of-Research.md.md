- Summary:: "The Craft of Research, Fourth Edition" seeks to provide guidance for researchers at different levels. The book emphasizes the importance of online resources in research and advocates for critical assessment of sources, particularly secondary sources that constitute the scholarly conversation. The book also offers practical suggestions for engaging with sources and constructing arguments, as well as tips for citing sources effectively. The fourth edition features updated content and a standardized approach to academic and professional genres.
