- Summary:: The text discusses the need for a new approach to science by creating smaller, independent research groups called Science Houses. These houses offer young scientists freedom, guidance, and funding to pursue innovative research outside of traditional academia. By investing in Science Houses, more science can be conducted for less money, empowering talented individuals to explore their potential.

- Every Little Ship should have its own founding ideas, and these are mine:
  1. Learning how to do science is a mysterious, ineffable process where you must spend a lot of time hanging out with someone who knows the ropes and other people who also want to know the ropes. It is only through this process that you can hone your intuitions, your most precious research abilities.
  2. Science must be expressed honestly, in the voice of the person who did the work, in words that any interested person can understand, and in a place where they can access it. It is only through this freedom that you can fully cultivate your ideas. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hyry7g0tykj1tb854jgc2hme))
