- Document Note: N of 300 korean university students in seoul
- Summary:: This study examined how academic self-efficacy, stress, and performance expectations relate to AI dependency among university students. It found that low academic self-efficacy can lead to higher academic stress, which in turn increases reliance on AI. The research highlights the negative impacts of excessive AI use, such as decreased creativity and increased laziness.

- Contrary to our predictions, there was no strong association between academic self-efficacy and AI dependency. This is inconsistent with previous studies (Li et al., 2020; Odaci, 2011; Parmaksız, 2022), indicating that low academic efficacy does not necessarily lead to prob- lematic behavior among students. However, this study identified an indirect connection between academic self-efficacy and AI dependency mediated by academic stress. There- fore, although academic self-efficacy does not directly affect students’ problematic AI usage behavior, students with low academic self-efficacy still rely on AI when they are under aca- demic pressure. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01je4ghsz9s9qsaef4a3xeszep))
- However, a significant relationship between perfor- mance expectations and AI dependency was found. Consistent with the results of Dwivedi et al. (2019) and Nan et al.
