- Summary:: The study examines the effects of kindergarten retention on children's growth in reading and math. It finds that retained students generally perform worse than their promoted peers, suggesting that retention hinders cognitive development. Overall, the research indicates that kindergarten retention does not improve learning outcomes for at-risk children and may leave them further behind.
