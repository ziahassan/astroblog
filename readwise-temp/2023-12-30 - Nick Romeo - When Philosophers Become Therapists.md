These sessions involve discussing personal dilemmas with the aim of posing philosophical questions and exploring different philosophical approaches to find insights and solutions. Philosophical counselling is seen as a form of personal tutoring in philosophy, offering a unique perspective and increasing one's capacity for rational assessment. The goal is to spark transformation by exposing individuals to various viewpoints and helping them develop a deeper understanding of themselves and their values.
