## Highlights
- The allure of instant, effortless content is undeniable. With a few keystrokes, we can summon walls of text, lists of ideas, or pseudo-profound insights. But let's be clear: this isn't progress. It's intellectual junk food, offering a momentary sugar rush of productivity with no lasting nourishment.
  I call it "AI slop" (as many do) – not because it's created by AI, but because we humans are using AI as an excuse to lower our standards. We're churning out vapid, generic content that serves no purpose beyond its own creation. It's a parlour trick masquerading as innovation.
