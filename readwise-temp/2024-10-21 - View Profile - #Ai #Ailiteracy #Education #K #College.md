## Highlights
- Sure, teach students how to use the tools and what to be wary of. That won't take much schooling time, but it also won't have much durable value. The tools will change, and students can't spot danger without other thinking skills that are in short supply even in adult populations. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaqy1tdy7ay1rcmwsxnj0xcs))
- The beauty of teaching "AI Thinking"-about distributed systems and patterns, learning and teaching, adapting, socializing, controlling, approaches to common problems, ai and human cognitive warts-is that it's really about meta-thinking. That's why it's so important. It teaches about important skills around judgment, problem solving, management, and relationships that fit into any course. (I truly believe nearly any learning module in K-12 can be used to make an AI point too, especially by comparing AI to human skill.) Teachers can already talk about these metathinking topics with students, AI or not. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaqy27zpjsqgb2eq28e4y9pg))
- AI is a gateway to principles of human thinking and learning, to system and computational thinking, to data science and engineering if desired, and to a sort of applied ethics. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jaqy3h4ma7thd1k0pzpanfmj))
