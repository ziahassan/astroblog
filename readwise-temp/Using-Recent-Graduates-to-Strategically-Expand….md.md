- Summary:: The COVID-19 pandemic has worsened educational inequities, particularly for low-income and minority students. Expanding AmeriCorps can provide essential support, such as tutoring and college transition help, to prevent a lost generation of students. Engaging recent graduates in these programs can effectively address these challenges and benefit communities long-term.
