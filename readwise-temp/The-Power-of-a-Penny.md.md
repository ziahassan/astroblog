- Summary:: The federal government spends less than one-tenth of a penny on evaluating education programs, which limits effective use of taxpayer money. The report suggests that investing just 1% of education funding in evaluations could improve results and help solve ongoing education problems. It provides eight recommendations for building a strong evaluation system to enhance educational outcomes for all children.
