## Highlights
- *Gen Z is* indeed *changing* English, but it is also very much a misconception that this constitutes a degradation or ruination of the language. Throughout history, it has always been the younger generations that drive language change, and then grow old enough to complain about kids in their own time! ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h78xnakj2vesbxgjqr1ycv5x))
- Such complainers seem to miss (pun intended) the simple fact that they were once young too, and just as responsible for “changing the language” as today’s youths are. No generation has the distinction of having “finally figured English out,” such that subsequent generations’ deviations constitute a dumbing-down or degradation of the language!
